# HoloAssist: what follows routine instructor speech during fine-motor work?

Generated by `python -m scripts.holoassist_speech_cost`. Labels only. 1454 videos, 18649 routine instructor onsets (next-step, follow-up, confirmation). Chance level: per-video circular shift of the onsets, 500 repetitions. 95% interval: 2000 resamples of videos. See the script docstring for the definitions and the main caveat (a pause of the performer can cause the speech).

- end_soon: the action under the onset ends within 1.5 s
- resumed: the same verb and noun start again within 10 s after that action ends
- mistake: a wrong action starts within 15 s after the onset
