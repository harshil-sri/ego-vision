# HoloAssist pilot part 2b: hand-state effect adjusted for need proxies

Generated by `python -m scripts.holoassist_adjusted`. 818401 ticks of 0.5 s from 1568 videos, 18389 routine instructor onsets. Odds ratio = odds of a routine onset in the next 0.5 s in this hand state, divided by the odds with idle hands. Adjusted = at equal values of four need proxies (time since the instructor last spoke, time since the last coarse step boundary, time in task, fraction of task elapsed; spline terms). Within video = the same, plus each video's own overall onset rate as a covariate, so quiet and talkative sessions are not compared with each other. This is the column to trust. Interval (for the within-video column) = central 95% of 40 refits on videos resampled with replacement.
