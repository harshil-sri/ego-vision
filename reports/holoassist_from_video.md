# HoloAssist pilot part 5: the same questions, from video

Generated by `python -m scripts.holoassist_from_video`. 305 videos from 64 recording sessions (a random subset, whole sessions), 79280 ticks of 1.0 s. Video = one frozen VideoMAE vector per tick (last 2.0 s). All splits by session, 5 folds; hand-state predictions are cross-fitted. See the script docstring for the design.
